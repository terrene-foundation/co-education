# Research Methods

Practical guidance on conducting academic research -- from evaluating whether a source is credible, to collecting data from major repositories and databases, to applying basic quantitative techniques, to synthesizing findings from multiple studies. Designed for UG/PG students undertaking research projects, honours theses, or dissertations in any discipline.

## Quick Reference

1. **CRAAP Test**: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose -- five criteria for evaluating sources
2. **Key Repositories**: Government statistics portals, institutional data archives, open-access databases, disciplinary repositories
3. **OLS Assumptions**: Linearity, no perfect multicollinearity, exogeneity, homoscedasticity, no autocorrelation, normality of errors
4. **Quasi-Experimental Methods**: Difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity, instrumental variables -- for causal inference from observational data
5. **Thematic Synthesis**: Organize literature by themes/concepts, not by author or chronology
